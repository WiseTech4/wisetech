BNHS is a public Secondary School in Camarines Norte, Philippines, providing a quality education for both Junior and Senior High School students. This school foster academic excellence, discipline and values formation, preparing learners to be competent, responsible and future-ready oriented. Also, Basud nurture both academic and sports program where every learners has a choice to choose between taking the academic track or sports track.

Nurturing the truth tellers

Special Program in Journalism (SPJ)

BNHS implemented the Special Program in Journalism (SPJ) for School Year 2025–2026, catering to Grade 7 learners who show interest and potential in the field of journalism. The program aims to strengthen students’ knowledge, skills, and values in various areas of journalism. With the guidance of their adviser, Ma’am Merly M. Retig, learners are trained in news writing, feature writing, editorial writing, and other forms of journalistic expression. The SPJ is designed to provide comprehensive learning experiences, starting from basic concepts and gradually progressing to advanced journalistic skills. Through this program, BNHS seeks to develop critical thinkers, effective communicators, and responsible campus journalists who are prepared to participate in school, division, and regional journalism activities.

WHERE THE TRUTH LIES. BNHS publishes two official school publications to serve its English and Filipino mediums of instruction: The Horizon for the English medium and Abot-Tanaw for the Filipino medium.”

The Horizon

Standing on the Pillars of Truth

The official English-language student publication of BNHS. The Horizon runs for nearly a decades now with the guidance of their School Paper Adviser (SPA) Ma'am Merly M. Retig. It demonstrated how committed they are when it comes to tell the truth, responsible reporting, and dissemination of relevant issues within the community of Basud, Camarines Norte. The Publication consistently represents the school into different journalistic competition, from Division level up to the National Level. Upholding the pride and passion in journalism.

Abot-Tanaw

Sandigan ng Karunungan, Tanggulan ng Katotohanan.

The official Filipino-language student publication of BNHS, Abot-Tanaw, was established in 2020. However, the first issue of the paper was published only in 2025, under the leadership of Editor-in-Chief Dylenne Zoe Arcega with the guidance of their Schoo Paper Adviser (SPA) Sir Ethan Ferr P. Galvan. Throughout the year, all staff members of Abot-Tanaw actively competed in various categories of journalism. They are committed to reporting the truth to the people of Basud, providing timely and chronological updates on national and local issues. The publication consistently represents the school in journalistic competitions, from the Division level up to the National level, upholding the pride and passion of every journalist. Both publications have left a lasting legacy throughout their existence.
